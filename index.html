The Person Behind the Data

I grew up watching economic theory explain the world around me — why some communities thrived and others didn't, why certain decisions compounded into crises. Economics gave me a lens. Data gave me a microscope.

My background sits at an unusual intersection: I trained as an economist, spent years in academic research and data management, and then made a deliberate pivot into data analytics — because I wanted to apply that analytical rigour to problems where the stakes were real and the decisions were immediate.

"I look at datasets the way central banks look at inflation — constantly monitoring the variables, hunting down anomalies, and making sure the risk doesn't break the system."

Today I build end-to-end analytics pipelines: from raw, messy data through structured databases and into interactive dashboards that answer the question every stakeholder actually cares about — so what do we do about it?

My niche is credit risk and financial analytics, but my toolkit and curiosity stretch across domains. I'm open to wherever good data problems live — fintech, operations, commercial analytics, public sector.

Credit Risk Analytics —
Lending Club Portfolio

The business question: where is the risk concentrated in a 11-year, $30B+ lending portfolio — and how has it evolved during aggressive growth? I built the answer from scratch: ingested 30M+ raw records, architected an 8-table Star Schema in MySQL, and delivered a 5-page Power BI dashboard with actionable risk intelligence for institutional decision-makers.

Identified 21.57% concluded default rate — more than 1 in 5 loans reaching final state resulted in loss
Proved geography as an independent risk driver: Mississippi (29.25%) vs Washington DC (14.40%) at near-identical FICO scores
Isolated Grade G as a negative expected value segment — 52.2% default rate erodes the 28.07% interest yield
Quantified 2× portfolio quality decay as annual issuance grew from 134K to 495K loans (2013–2018)

Operational Safety · Public Sector · Power BI

UK Road Accident Analysis —
195K Casualties

Road safety data is often reported. Rarely analysed. I built a decision-support dashboard for transport policymakers to understand not just what happened, but where interventions are working — and where they aren't.

Quantified 11.9% YoY casualty reduction with 35.6% drop in fatal incidents — proving interventions were working at the severe end
Identified single carriageways (145K casualties) and urban environments (61.95%) as primary unresolved risk vectors
Surfaced the "Daylight Paradox" — 73.84% of casualties occur in daylight, pointing to driver behaviour over visibility as the real problem
